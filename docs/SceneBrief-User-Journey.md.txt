# User journey

The EMT talks while treating; SceneBrief checks every claim against the patient's wearables and sends the ER a verified brief before the ambulance arrives. Times follow the heat stroke demo.

## Journey map

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  P["Earlier: patient links Apple Watch + WHOOP and opts in"]
  subgraph EMT
    E1["10:55 Arrives on scene<br/>Scans the opt-in code"]
    E2["10:58 Narrates the scene<br/>Talks while treating"]
    E3["11:00 Confirms or corrects<br/>By voice, on the spot"]
    E4["Transport: Photographs med list<br/>Starts transport"]
  end
  subgraph SceneBrief
    S1["Loads wearable data<br/>Last 24 h, Apple + WHOOP"]
    S2["Checks each claim<br/>Against wearable timeline"]
    S3["Flags 4 claims<br/>Prompts temp, glucose, ECG"]
    S4["Sends brief to ER<br/>Before ambulance arrives"]
  end
  subgraph ER["ER team"]
    R1["At the ER: Reviews the handoff<br/>Known, changed, uncertain<br/>Taps items to see source"]
  end
  P -.-> S1
  E1 --> S1
  E1 --> E2
  E2 --> S2
  S1 --> S2
  S2 --> S3
  S3 --> E3
  E3 --> E4
  E4 --> S4
  S4 --> R1
```

The one loop that matters is SceneBrief flagging claims and the EMT confirming or correcting them, all before the ER sees the brief.

## Pain points it removes

| Stage | Pain point today | With SceneBrief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before the call | Wearable history is locked on the patient's phone | Patient opts in ahead of time, like Medical ID |
| Arrival | EMT starts with only a stressed witness's account | 24 h of Apple Watch and WHOOP data loads when the EMT scans the opt-in code |
| Assess and narrate | Notes are written later, from memory | EMT talks while treating; speech becomes claims |
| Flag and clarify | Wrong assumptions ("sudden collapse," "down 5 minutes") go unchecked | Conflicts are flagged with their source; EMT confirms or corrects by voice; protocol prompts appear |
| Transport | The handoff is pieced together at the ER door | The brief reaches the ER before the ambulance does |
| ER handoff | Verbal handoff loses detail and uncertainty | One screen: known, changed, uncertain, each item linked to its source |
